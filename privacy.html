Privacy notice

How AgileTalent Associates Limited handles information you share with us.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

AgileTalent Associates Limited provides human resources consulting, recruitment and workforce solutions. For privacy enquiries, contact us at agiletalents@outlook.com.

2. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect your name, organisation, email address, telephone number, organisation size, the service you are interested in and the message you provide. We may also receive basic technical information associated with your use of the website.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to enquiries, understand your organisation's needs, discuss our services, prepare proposals and maintain records of business communications.

4. Consent and lawful processing

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Depending on the circumstances, we may also process information where necessary to take steps at your request, pursue legitimate business interests or meet legal obligations. We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection law.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may use service providers to operate our website, receive enquiries or manage business communications. These providers may process information on our behalf. We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through this form. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect our legal rights.

6. Storage and retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, including handling enquiries, maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the nature of the information and the circumstances.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

9. Third-party services and external links

Our enquiry form uses Google Forms to receive and record submissions. Google may process information under its own privacy terms. Our website may also link to external services; their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.

11. Contact us

For questions or requests concerning personal information, contact AgileTalent Associates Limited:

Email: agiletalents@outlook.com

Phone: +234 703 678 3875

WhatsApp: Chat with us on WhatsApp

This notice is general website privacy information and is not a substitute for a review of the company's specific data processing practices and legal obligations.
