Recruitment and Talent Acquisition

The challenge
Hiring takes managers away from their real work, and a poor hire is expensive to correct.
How we help
We run recruitment on your behalf: sourcing, screening, interview scheduling, candidate communication and shortlisting. Our database includes 4,000+ pre-screened candidates, with suitability assessed against each role’s requirements. We support one-off roles, recurring hiring and volume recruitment, with activities agreed for each engagement.
The benefit
Less administrative load on your team, better coordination between you and candidates, and a clearer basis for hiring decisions.
Next step
Share the roles and hiring context. We agree scope and responsibilities in writing before work begins.

HR Consulting and People Operations

The challenge
Growing businesses often run on informal HR practice, which creates inconsistency and avoidable disputes.
How we help
We design HR processes, write policies, procedures and employee handbooks, build documentation, and set up performance management and employee relations frameworks. We also support workforce planning and organisational structuring.
The benefit
Consistent people practices, clearer manager expectations and fewer avoidable people issues.
Next step
An HR health check of what you have today, followed by a prioritised plan.

SME Payroll and HR Administration

The challenge
Late or inaccurate pay damages employee trust, and weak records make PAYE and other statutory obligations harder to manage.
How we help
We support payroll administration and processing, employee records management, payroll reporting and reconciliation, and the HR documentation that sits behind them.
The benefit
Reliable, repeatable payroll cycles and records you can rely on.
Next step
We review your current payroll process and agree what we take on. Statutory positions should be confirmed with your tax adviser or the relevant authority.

Performance Management and Workforce Effectiveness

The challenge
When goals are unclear and feedback is rare, performance reviews become a yearly formality instead of a management tool.
How we help
We design performance frameworks, support KPI and goal setting, build review processes, develop managers' capability, and help you report on workforce data.
The benefit
Clear accountability, better conversations between managers and teams, and people decisions backed by evidence.
Next step
A short diagnostic of how performance is managed today, then a framework sized to your organisation.

Learning and Development

The challenge
Capability gaps, especially among first-time managers, slow teams down and are rarely fixed by generic training.
How we help
We run training needs analysis, shape learning strategy, and deliver leadership, management and capability programmes. We evaluate whether training changes how work is done.
The benefit
Learning tied to real workplace gaps, with a way to judge whether it worked.
Next step
We start with the capability gap, not a course catalogue.
